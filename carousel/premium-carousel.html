Marketing — Editorial
Your API PlaybookStrategy Session

Cool Marketing
That Actually Works

→

Prioritize Developers Over Features

Developers choose APIs based on trust, docs quality, and how fast they can prototype. Lead with DX, not features.

→

Share Stories, Not Sales Pitches

"We built this because our football app needed live data and nothing existed." That's a story. "Buy our API" is noise.

→

Build Trust Prior to Making a Sale

Offer a generous free tier. Publish transparent status pages. Respond to support tickets in under 2 hours.

→

Assess What Truly Counts

1,000 free users and 50 paying customers beats 10,000 followers. Focus on conversion, not vanity metrics.
