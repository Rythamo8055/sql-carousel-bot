## 🔄 Content Repurposing Flow

```

├── X Thread (10 tweets)
├── Threads Multi-post (5 posts)

├── Instagram Reel (30 sec)
```

## 💡 Pro Tips for Multi-Platform

### Consistency
1. **Same branding** across all platforms
2. **Same voice** and tone
3. **Cross-promote** but don't spam
4. **Repurpose** content smartly

### Time Management
1. **Batch create** content
2. **Schedule** in advance
3. **Use templates** for each platform
4. **Focus on 2 platforms** first, then expand

### Engagement
1. **Reply to every comment** on all platforms
2. **Ask questions** to spark discussion
3. **Feature community** across platforms
4. **Be authentic** everywhere
